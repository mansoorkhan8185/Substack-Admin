Privacy Policy

Substack Reader’s · Last updated: November 7, 2026

Substack Reader’s (“the app”) is an unofficial reader that opens the Substack website inside the app. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Substack. This policy explains what information the app and the services it uses may collect.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account with us, and we do not collect your name, email address or reading history ourselves. When you browse or sign in, that happens on Substack’s website and is covered by Substack’s privacy policy.

Services used by the app

  • Firebase (Google): Remote Config delivers app settings such as the app name or maintenance messages, and Analytics may collect an app instance identifier, device model, operating system version and basic usage data. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Unity Ads and Google AdMob: the app shows advertising. These providers may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads. See Unity’s privacy policy and Google’s policy above. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device’s Android settings.

How information is used

Information collected by these services is used to run the app, show and measure ads, and understand technical problems. We do not sell personal information.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security and retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Data held by the third-party services above is kept according to their own policies.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version at this address with a new date.
